04 / BEFORE YOU DOWNLOAD

Built for a classic.
Still being refined.

This is an independent development beta for the first-generation Axe-Fx Ultra. The manual records what works, what was tested and what remains experimental.

Read the legacy feature audit ↗
The Mac
Apple Silicon. macOS 13 or later is the build target; hardware testing used macOS 27.0.
The hardware
Axe-Fx Ultra, tested on firmware 11.00 through a Clarett 8Pre. Standard, II, III and FM devices are outside scope.
The evidence
Preset reads, representative edits, bank backup and Store/restore were tested on hardware. Tuner, tempo commands and direct cabinet transfer still need hardware validation. Read the test record.
Your workspace
Local library, snapshots and drafts. No account or cloud service required. Existing Ultra Edit data stays in place after the rename.
The release
Ad-hoc signed, not Apple-notarized. Follow the first-launch guide. Firmware updating is deliberately excluded.
